Big Five Personality Assessment

A quick, science-based look at your personality across five core traits.

This assessment uses the public-domain 50-item IPIP Big-Five Factor Markers (Goldberg, 1992). You'll rate 50 short statements about yourself, presented in random order, on a scale from 1 to 7.

  • 50 questions · about 5–7 minutes
  • Answer honestly — there are no right or wrong answers
  • Rate how much each statement describes you, from Strongly Disagree (1) to Strongly Agree (7)
  • At the end you'll get a score and description for each of the five traits

This is an educational self-reflection tool, not a clinical or diagnostic instrument. Your answers stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Your personality profile

Each score is the average of your answers for that trait, on a scale of 1 (low) to 7 (high).

How to read this: Scores from 1.0–3.0 are Low, 3.1–5.0 are Moderate, and 5.1–7.0 are High. No score is "good" or "bad" — each trait is a spectrum, and every point on it has strengths.

Based on the IPIP Big-Five Factor Markers (Goldberg, 1992), a public-domain instrument. Educational use only — not a clinical assessment.
